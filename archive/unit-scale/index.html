Unit scale

Move units across a map and fight.

Build a custom battle, as in Total War. Choose the armies and the ground. Then arrange your units, plan their routes, and start. Pause at any time to plan or give orders.

The fight

The same seed and the same orders give the same battle. The seed is shown during the battle.

Armies

Numbers are dots. Each dot is one unit of people, sized by the scale. Tap a number, then scroll to change it. The computer plans every team that is not yours.

AttackerDefenderThird

Your units are arranged by role when the battle is set up: the front line, the archers behind, the cavalry on the flanks.

Third team and allies

Allies share what they see and win or lose together. A team is out when it has no units left, or when it is down to a twentieth of its enemies' strength.

Battlefield generator

Ground, roads and rules for this battle. The same choices are saved with the replay.

Ground

Makes new ground. Your armies and orders stay the same.

Sites

Leave out any of these to clear them from the ground.

Rules

Deployment. Place your units inside the blue strip, then begin.

Formations

Up to two formations. Give one command to a formation, and every unit keeps its place in it.
