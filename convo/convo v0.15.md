## Direction from Jimmy

- Sometimes **all the teams move together as one** to attack a single place.
- They **should work together, and not kill each other.**
- Because they are **separate nations**, they carry a **slight penalty**.

## What is built (defaults I picked, to check)

- **Joining:** every ally that is at war with the target, and can reach it, joins the attack. The lead nation's attack is pooled with theirs: one army, one battle, three waves.
- **No friendly fire:** allies are not at war with each other, so they never fight. Their troops pass each other's land freely, as before.
- **Each sends its share:** every nation sends the lead's share (the attack's percentage) of its own soldiers, and pays its own march in gold and food and its own magic.
- **Penalty:** 5% less strength for each nation beyond the first, at most 20% less. Two nations: 5%. Four nations: 15%.
- **One joint attack a turn:** a nation that has already attacked this turn does not join a second attack. Its allies' second order is logged and skipped.
- **Survivors go home** to the nation that sent them, in proportion to what each sent. A captured province goes to the lead nation.
- **The card** shows which allies will join your attack, and the penalty.
- **Computer nations** use the same rule, so AI allies also join each other's attacks.
- **Version 2.17** in ⚙️.

## Limits of this build

- The joint army defends and attacks through the lead's entry province. An ally that reaches the target through a different province still fights there.
- An ally is only pulled in when it is already at war with the target. Declaring war for an ally is not automatic.
- The War plan's own attack check still looks at the lead nation alone, so it does not count allies yet.
