# Expansion Wars — Convo v0.18 (version 2.20: food by hand, soldiers first, land caps the army)

**Round:** 19. Direction from Jimmy, built with the defaults below for you to check.

## Direction from Jimmy

- **Food top-up should be manual.**
- **Soldiers eat first.** Food is the top line: a map of a given size can only feed a certain number of soldiers.

## What is built (defaults I picked, to check)

- **Manual food.** Buy 10 food for 20 gold, or for 30 stone, with the buttons on your card. Nothing is bought for you. Computer nations buy food themselves when they run short (gold first, then stone), so they do not starve for lack of a rule.
- **Soldiers eat first.** Each soldier eats 0.05 food a turn. Whatever is left goes to the people (0.5 each). If the army cannot be fed, soldiers starve (3% a turn, as before). If the people cannot be fed, unrest rises by 2 a turn.
- **The land caps the army.** A nation can feed 14 soldiers per province. The Arms track can lift the cap, but never above what the land feeds. Recruiting stops at the cap. Existing soldiers are not cut to it.
- **Version 2.20** in ⚙️.

## What the simulation showed (six maps, 40 turns, no hand moves)

- **Growth:** population about 69, about 34 provinces, army about 430, food about 615. Land allows a big army here, so food is still not scarce for Growth.
- **War:** about 2.5 provinces, about 32 soldiers, one fall in six. The land is small, so the army is small. This is the land cap at work, and War's attacks now need land to pay for an army.
- **Wealth:** about 41 population, about 12 provinces, army about 164, food about 560.
