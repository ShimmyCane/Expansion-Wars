# Expansion Wars — Convo v0.10 (version 2: world, conquest and water routes)

**Project:** Expansion Wars
**Round:** 11. Answers from Jimmy go inline under each question.

## Jimmy's direction (this round)

- **A bigger world.** More city states and countries, which grow and expand on their own.
- **Defeat absorbs, not just removes.** When a country is defeated, its land and its people (talents) move into yours. You take the land but keep managing it from the same place: one country screen, one set of armies.
- **Water is water.** A lake or sea is shown as solid water, not a dashed line. Dashed lines are for roads and routes only.
- **Water routes.** Ships can carry trade across water.
- **Trade range.** Trade is limited to adjacent countries, or to countries joined by a water route. Distance is a factor in both the capacity of a trade route and the benefit it gives.

## My reading

- A conquered country's regions join your country list. Its population, talents and stone go to you when it is taken. Its soldiers either join your army or are lost (open question 1).
- "Dashed line" today means a road between two spots. A water crossing would be drawn as solid blue, and a water route as a separate ship lane.
- Trade partner rule: (a) the two countries share a land border, or (b) both have a port on the same water body. Distance then sets how much a route can carry (capacity) and how much it pays (benefit). Shorter routes carry more and pay less per unit, longer routes carry less and pay more. Exact numbers to be set after a test.
- Suggested order: settle the resource rules (question 6), then build the bigger map, then conquest and water routes.
