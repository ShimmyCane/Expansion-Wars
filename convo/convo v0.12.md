# Expansion Wars — Convo v0.12 (drifting borders: answers to v0.11)

**Project:** Expansion Wars
**Round:** 13. Answers from Jimmy to convo v0.11 are recorded here.

## Answers to convo v0.11

1. **Drift speed.** A share of the border, relative to the attacking army's strength. Each population centre costs the attacker a set number of soldiers, and up to one third of the attacking force can turn into border moves.
   - *Still open:* the soldiers per population centre, and how the border share maps to moves.

2. **Start counts.** The count depends on the country's size.
   - *Still open:* the formula (for example, dots per spot, or dots per share of the map).

3. **Unrest threshold.** Unrest (the (un)happiness scale) rises with:
   - long wars (10 or more turns),
   - multiple wars at once,
   - too little butter (food),
   - too little trade,
   - alliances between nations with poor reputation.
   - *Still open:* the unrest level at which a spot revolts, and whether a revolt is certain at the threshold or chance-based.

4. **Attack costs under unrest.** Both: a percentage and a flat number of extra soldiers.
   - *Still open:* the size of each.

5. **Zoom.** One dot per population centre. Once a centre is upgraded, it shows as a town or a city, not a plain dot.

## Direction from this round

- Opponents should plan their turns carefully: when to attack, when to make peace, when to develop, and when to gang up on a strong nation.
- That favours a planning view that shows the options each turn, which will need its own design round.
