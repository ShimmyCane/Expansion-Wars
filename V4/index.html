Build a custom battle, as in Total War. Choose the armies and the ground. Then place your units, and the battle runs in real time. Pause at any time to give orders.

The fight

The same seed and the same orders give the same battle. The seed is shown during the battle.

Armies

Numbers are dots. Each dot is one unit of people, sized by the scale. The defender is planned by the computer.

Applies to your units when the battle starts. You can change any unit during the battle.
